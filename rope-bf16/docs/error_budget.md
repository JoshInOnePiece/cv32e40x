# Strict-BF16 RoPE — Error Budget

Generated by `model/characterize.py` (d=128, base=10000).

Definitions:

* **Model A** — strict BF16, rounded RNE at every node. This is the hardware (INV-1).
* **Model B** — BF16 in/out, FP32 internal accumulate (`AccWidth=32`, measurement only).
* **Model C** — FP64 with libm `sin`/`cos`. The yardstick.
* **Pair-relative error** — `|(x',y')_A - (x',y')_C| / |(x',y')_C|`, i.e. error
  normalised by the pair norm. Preferred over per-component relative error, which
  is unbounded when a component's true value is near zero under cancellation.

**Cost of INV-1 (strict vs wide), RMS pair error: 1.266x.** Strict BF16 and wide accumulate produce bit-identical results on 45.1745% of these random cases.

Interpretation: for generic operands the dominant error term is the shared LUT quantisation plus the final BF16 rounding of the result, which both models pay. The extra intermediate roundings that INV-1 accepts contribute comparatively little away from cancellation — which is why the cancellation study below is the measurement that matters.

## 4. Catastrophic cancellation (the headline measurement)

`x' = x*cos - y*sin`. At 45 degrees `cos == sin`, so with `x == y` the two products cancel exactly and the true result is 0. Phase swept +/-2 degrees around 45 degrees in 20001 steps.

| operands | model | max rel err | RMS rel err | min bits kept | mean bits kept | frac. losing >half |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| x==y | strict BF16 | 1.478e+00 | 3.491e-01 | 0.0 | 3.5 | 63.2468% |
| x==y | wide acc | 1.000e+00 | 3.322e-01 | 0.0 | 3.7 | 59.5170% |
| y=1.01x | strict BF16 | 4.516e+02 | 3.745e+00 | 0.0 | 3.6 | 62.1719% |
| y=1.01x | wide acc | 5.568e+02 | 4.479e+00 | 0.0 | 3.7 | 58.8771% |
| y=1.1x | strict BF16 | 3.699e-01 | 6.041e-02 | 1.4 | 5.2 | 22.3489% |
| y=1.1x | wide acc | 1.993e-01 | 4.841e-02 | 2.3 | 5.4 | 16.5042% |

'bits kept' is `-log2(relative error)`, clipped to [0, 24]; 'frac. losing >half' is the fraction of cases retaining fewer than 4 of BF16's 8 significand bits.
